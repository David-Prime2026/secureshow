The problem

Physical keys and shared codes are liabilities

Traditional property access methods expose agents, sellers, and property managers to unverified visitors, zero accountability, and institutional risk.

Anonymous access

Lockbox codes are shared via text and group chats with no verification of who actually enters the property.

Zero accountability

No record of arrival, departure, or duration. When something goes wrong, there is no evidence trail.

Coordination overhead

Agents spend six to eight hours per listing managing showing requests, distributing codes, and confirming access.

Seller anxiety

Homeowners leave their property hoping the agent locked the door, hoping no one stayed too long, hoping nothing is missing.

Compliance gaps

Brokerages have no institutional-grade audit trail. When insurance or regulatory questions arise, the answer is a spiral notebook.

Buyer friction

Prospective buyers are constrained by agent availability. Evening, weekend, and early morning showings are lost opportunities.

The solution

Closed-loop verification from identity to departure

SecureShow transforms property access from a liability into a competitive advantage through identity verification, camera verification, and an immutable audit trail.

01

Identity verification

Government-issued ID validation with liveness detection confirms every visitor is a real, verified person before they receive access credentials.

02

Camera-verified access

Facial recognition at the property entrance confirms the verified individual is physically present. The person at the door is the person who passed identity verification.

03

Time-bound credentials

Digital access credentials are single-use, device-bound, and valid only within narrow time windows. Overstay triggers automated escalation.

04

Immutable audit trail

Every access event is recorded in an append-only log with hash-chain integrity, providing tamper-evident evidence for compliance, insurance, and dispute resolution.

How it works

Nine-step closed-loop verification

From identity verification to departure confirmation, every step is verified, recorded, and auditable.

1

Download & register

Install the SecureShow app and register your device with location, camera, and notification permissions.

2

Verify identity

Government-issued ID scan with liveness detection confirms you are a real, verified person.

3

Accept liability

Review and digitally sign the property access liability agreement with device and location binding.

4

Schedule access

Request a showing within available time windows. The property owner or agent approves.

5

Receive credential

A time-bound, single-use, device-bound digital credential is issued for your approved window.

6

Arrive & verify

The doorbell camera performs facial recognition, confirming you match your verified identity.

7

Access property

Tap unlock in the app. GPS confirms you are within the geofence. The smart lock opens.

8

Depart & confirm

Camera verifies departure and person-count reconciliation ensures everyone who entered has left.

9

Audit & report

Complete access record with timestamps, durations, camera evidence, and hash-chain integrity.
